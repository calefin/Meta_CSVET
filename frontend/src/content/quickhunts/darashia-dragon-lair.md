## Why go
- Classic dragon spawn close to a depot, no access quest.
- Good loot for the level: dragon shields, green dragon leather, dragon ham.

## Bring
- Fire protection helps a lot (dragon fire wave and fireballs).
- Health potions for knights; mana potions for mages and paladins.

## Route tips
- Clear the small lairs in a loop; respawn is short.
- The occasional Dragon Lord appears on the deeper floor: lure carefully.
