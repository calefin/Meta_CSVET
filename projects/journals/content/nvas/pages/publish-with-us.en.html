Frequently asked questions

Do I have to pay to publish?

No. There are no submission or publication fees for manuscripts accepted until 31 January 2027. See the fee policy in Section 7 of the Guide for Authors.

I am a student. Can I publish?

Yes. Students are welcome, with their supervisor or another graduate professional as the co-author responsible for the work.

Which languages are accepted?

Manuscripts may be submitted entirely in English or entirely in Portuguese. NVAS publishes in English: if a manuscript in Portuguese is accepted, the journal takes care of the translation, and the authors review and approve it before publication. Text inside figures and graphs must be in English from the start.

What is the best article type for a first paper?

Case reports and clinical images. A well-documented case with a confirmed diagnosis already makes a good paper. The templates show what to write in each section.

Can my thesis become a paper?

Yes, as long as it is rewritten as an article in the template for the chosen type: shorter, direct and following the journal's structure. Theses in their original format are not accepted.

How long does it take?

Editorial screening usually takes up to 7 days. Then at least two reviewers assess the work, and you can follow every step in the journal's dashboard.

Hablo español. ¿Puedo publicar?

Sí. Los manuscritos se envían en inglés o en portugués, y puede pedirnos el contacto en español: elija «Español» en el formulario.
