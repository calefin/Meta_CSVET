Your undergraduate, specialisation or residency monograph, master's dissertation or doctoral thesis can become an open-access book with an ISBN, peer reviewed and indexed in Google Scholar. Here is how it works and how everyone involved is credited.

  • ISBN and cataloguing recordIssued by CBL, with legal deposit at the National Library of Brazil.
  • No submission or review feesEditorial decisions never depend on payment.
  • Open access and visibilityBook page indexed in Google Scholar and Google Books.
  • Correct creditsAuthorship, supervision and examining committee, each in its place.

Step by step

  1. Adapt the text to book format: remove academic-only elements, rewrite the introduction and conclusion for the reader, update references and give the book its own title. Use the book template.
  2. Prepare the documents: book proposal, authors' statement, supervisor's consent and the approval record.
  3. Submit in the Monographs or Dissertations and Theses series, registering each contributor with the right role (see below).
  4. Review: internal review by the Editorial Board (up to 15 days), then double-anonymous review by at least one external reviewer who did not sit on your examining committee (up to 30 days).
  5. Revisions, contract, production (copyediting, typesetting in 16×23 cm, series cover, proofs, ISBN) and open-access publication (CC BY 4.0).

Only contributors with the Author role appear as authors in the catalogue, the suggested citation, Google Scholar meta tags and structured data. Supervision and examining committee are shown on the book page in a separate box, noting that the book derives from an approved academic work.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need my supervisor's permission?

We ask for the Supervisor's Consent, which confirms the origin of the work and how the supervisor wishes to be credited.

Part of the work was published as an article. Can I still publish the book?

Yes, if the book expands the content, cites the article and the article's licence or the journal allows reuse.

How long does it take?

With a well-adapted text, 2 to 4 months from submission to publication.
