A practical guide from submission to publication: how to submit through the platform, how to register co-authors, volume editors and chapter authors, and what happens at each review stage.

  • One account, one submissionOne person submits on behalf of everyone and becomes the primary contact.
  • Everyone listedCo-authors and volume editors are registered as contributors; they do not need an account.
  • Two-stage reviewInternal (Editorial Board) and external (double-anonymous peer review).
  • All on the platformReviews, revisions, proofs and messages are recorded in the book's workflow.

Before you start

  1. Create an account (Register, top right), confirm your email and add your institution and ORCID iD to your profile.
  2. Choose the series and read the author guidelines.
  3. Prepare the files: proposal, full text without author names, high-resolution figures and signed statements (templates in the guidelines).

How the work is reviewed

  1. Screening by the editorial office (up to 7 days).
  2. Internal review by an Editorial Board member (up to 15 days).
  3. External double-anonymous review: 2 reviewers (new works, edited volumes chapter by chapter, manuals) or 1 (works already approved by an examining committee), within 30 days.
  4. Decision: accept, minor revisions, major revisions (new round) or decline, with the reviews attached.
  5. Responding: upload the revised text with changes highlighted and a point-by-point response letter (30 days for major, 15 for minor revisions).
  6. Appeals within 30 days to contato@csvet.com.br, reassessed by a Board member not involved in the decision.

After acceptance

Publishing contract, copyediting (your approval within 10 days), typesetting and PDF proofs (corrections only, within 10 days), ISBN and cataloguing record from CBL, open-access publication and legal deposit.

Frequently asked questions

We are two authors. Who submits?

Either of you. The submitter adds the other as Author, in cover order, and sets the primary contact.

We are three volume editors. Do we all need accounts?

No. One submits and registers the others as Volume editors; those who want platform access create an account and ask to be added as participants.

Can I learn who reviewed my work?

Not during review, which is double-anonymous. After publication, reviewers who agree may be named in the book's editorial credits, with their degree and institution.
