Editora CSVET follows the guidelines of COPE (Committee on Publication Ethics), the international reference for publication ethics. This page explains, in plain language, what that means for you as an author, volume editor, reviewer or editor.

  • IntegrityOriginal text, authentic data and every source cited.
  • Fair authorshipThose who wrote are authors; supervisors and examiners get the right credit.
  • Independent reviewDouble-anonymous, free of conflicts of interest and of any payment.
  • TransparencyErrors corrected openly: correction, expression of concern or retraction.

For authors

  • Originality. Submit only your own work, not previously published as a book. Academic works in repositories and parts published as articles are accepted if disclosed and cited.
  • Plagiarism and text recycling. Every book is screened for similarity. Copying text, ideas, figures or data without citation is plagiarism, including undisclosed reuse of your own work.
  • Authorship. Authors made a substantial contribution, drafted or critically revised the work, approved the final version and are accountable for it. Other contributors go in the acknowledgements.
  • Data and images. Never fabricate or alter data. Brightness and contrast may be adjusted for the whole image; removing, moving or inserting elements is not allowed. Keep the original files.
  • Animal and human research. Report ethics approval (CEUA/CEP), owner consent and permits.
  • Third-party rights. Images, tables and excerpts from other works require written permission or an open licence, with the source stated.
  • Artificial intelligence. AI cannot be an author. Disclose any use of generative AI; you are accountable for everything, including checking each reference.
  • Conflicts of interest and funding must be disclosed.

For volume editors

  • Invite authors for their expertise and manage deadlines and quality, but acceptance is not your decision alone: every chapter is peer reviewed by the publisher.
  • Chapters by the volume editors are reviewed by independent reviewers chosen by the publisher.
  • Make sure every chapter author signs the Chapter Author Agreement, and never change an author's text without their consent.

For reviewers

  • Confidentiality: do not share the work or upload it to AI tools.
  • Conflicts of interest: decline if you know the authors, work at the same institution, served on the examining committee of the original work or have an interest in the topic.
  • Constructive reports: assess the work, not the people; be specific, respectful and on time.

For editors and the Editorial Board

  • Decide on merit, scope and peer review only, regardless of nationality, institution, gender, belief or payment.
  • Declare conflicts and step aside from works by close colleagues, students or themselves.
  • Investigate suspected misconduct following COPE flowcharts, hearing the authors before any decision.

Works by the publisher's own editors

Editora CSVET's editors are also authors and volume editors. To give these works the same independent review as any other, at no cost and without relying on paid third parties, we follow COPE's recommended procedure:

  1. Independent handling editor: an Editorial Board member (volunteer) with no ties to the authors handles the submission; the editor-authors do not choose reviewers, read reviews before the decision or record the decision.
  2. Mandatory external review: at least two double-anonymous external reviews.
  3. Production may be done by the editors themselves after the decision: copyediting, typesetting, cover and publication do not affect merit.
  4. Transparency: the book carries an editorial note stating that the authors are part of the publisher and who handled the review.
  5. Limit: works by members of the publisher do not exceed 20% of titles published per year.

Use of our works: copyright, plagiarism and artificial intelligence

  • Open-access works (CC BY 4.0) may be copied, shared and adapted, including commercially, provided the authors, the work and Editora CSVET are credited and changes are indicated. Copying without credit is plagiarism and breaches the licence.
  • Commercial works (all rights reserved) may not be reproduced, distributed, sold, shared as files or used to train or feed artificial intelligence systems without written permission; short quotations with the source are allowed.
  • Unauthorised sale or distribution, or presenting our works as someone else's, infringes Brazilian Copyright Law No. 9,610/1998 and may be a crime.
  • Automated collection: AI training crawlers are blocked on our site, and commercial works carry a text and data mining (TDM) reservation. Licence requests: contato@csvet.com.br.

Corrections, retractions and complaints

  • Correction: fixes an error that does not invalidate the work; the previous version remains on record.
  • Expression of concern: public notice while a serious concern is investigated.
  • Retraction: when findings are unreliable or there was serious misconduct; the work stays online, marked as retracted, with the reason.
  • Complaints and appeals: write to contato@csvet.com.br. We reply within 15 working days; appeals are reassessed by a Board member not involved in the decision.

Frequently asked questions

Does my supervisor have to be a co-author?

No. Supervisors are credited as supervisors. They become co-authors only if they also wrote or critically revised the book version, approved the final text and accept accountability for it.

Can I use ChatGPT or other AI tools?

Yes, as support (for example, language editing), if you disclose the tool and its use, check every statement and reference, and do not list it as an author. AI-generated images are not accepted unless they are the subject of the text.

Do optional paid services affect acceptance?

No. There are no submission or review fees, and editorial decisions never depend on payment.

I found an error in a published book. What should I do?

Write to contato@csvet.com.br with the book, page and error. We assess it with the authors and publish a correction when appropriate.
