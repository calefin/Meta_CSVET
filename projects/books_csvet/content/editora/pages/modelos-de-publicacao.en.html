E-book formats

Under every model, the e-book is published in several versions, so readers can choose how they prefer to read:

VersionWhat forIdentifiers
Full PDFReading exactly like the print book, printing excerpts and citing page numbersOwn ISBN and the book DOI
EPUB 3Comfortable reading on phones, tablets and e-readers (Kindle, Kobo, Google Play Books, Apple Books), with adjustable font size, night mode and read-aloudOwn ISBN

Read onlineOpening the book right in the browser, with no downloadSame ISBN as the PDF

All e-books follow the accessibility rules in the Author Guidelines (alternative text for figures, heading structure and readable tables), so they can also be read by people with visual impairments (Brazilian Law 13,146/2015, art. 68). For commercial titles, each version is bought separately or as a bundle (PDF + EPUB), as set out in the contract.

How books are sold

  • On the publisher's portal: readers create an account, buy the format they want and download it from their account.
  • Print on demand: copies are printed per order by partner printers, with no stock and no upfront cost.
  • Digital stores such as Amazon Kindle and Google Play Books, linked from the book page.
  • Individual chapters of commercial edited volumes may be sold separately.

Moral rights always remain with the authors. Commercial works carry an all-rights-reserved notice, a prohibition on AI training and a text and data mining reservation. Each e-book sold is a personalised copy: the buyer's name and e-mail, order number, date and a verification code appear in the footer of every PDF page and on the EPUB credits page. Copies are for personal use; if a file is shared, the code identifies the original purchase. There is no reading lock (DRM): the file opens on any device. See Ethics and integrity.
