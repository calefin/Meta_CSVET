Have a topic and a network of experts? Propose an edited volume: you edit the book and choose the chapter authors, and Editora CSVET sends the invitations through the platform and handles peer review, production, ISBN and promotion.

  • Invitations through the platformEach author receives an email on your behalf, creates an account and delivers the chapter inside the book.
  • Citable chaptersEach chapter has its own page indexed in Google Scholar.
  • No cost for authorsNo submission, review or digital publication fees.
  • Editor creditYou appear as Volume editor on the cover, cataloguing record and metadata.

How it works

  1. Proposal: fill in the edited volume proposal and the chapter invitation spreadsheet (one author per row, with chapter title and deadline).
  2. Submit in the Edited Volumes series, mark it as an edited volume and register yourself as Volume editor.
  3. Proposal review by the Editorial Board (up to 15 days).
  4. Invitations are sent by the publisher, on your behalf. Authors accept, create an account and add their ORCID iD.
  5. Authors are added to the book with their chapter already in the table of contents, and use the book's Discussions area to talk to you and deliver files.
  6. Chapters are prepared with the chapter template and the signed Chapter Author Agreement.
  7. Double-anonymous peer review of each chapter, then production and open-access publication with ISBN.

Frequently asked questions

How many chapters are needed?

We recommend at least 6 chapters.

The invited author already has an account. What happens?

They sign in with their existing account and accept; they are then added to the book with their chapter.

Can a chapter have several authors?

Yes. Repeat the chapter title in the spreadsheet, one row per author.
