Veterinary hospitals and residency programmes see cases every day that deserve to be published. I would therefore like to invite your team to submit case reports, case series and clinical images to Neotropical Veterinary and Animal Science (NVAS), an open-access journal published by CSVET Editora (Brazil).

  • Case reports and case series: clinical, surgical, pathological or epidemiological cases with novelty or educational value, in any species;
  • Clinical images: one striking image (clinical, diagnostic imaging, gross or microscopic) with a brief educational description, a short format well suited to residents.

Manuscripts undergo double-anonymous peer review and follow the CARE guideline. There are no fees for manuscripts accepted until 31 January 2027, and articles are published open access (CC BY 4.0) as soon as they are accepted. At least one graduated author responsible for the case is required; routine cases with anonymized material need no consent form.

I would be grateful if you could share this invitation with residents and clinicians.
