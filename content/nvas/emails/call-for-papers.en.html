I am writing to invite you to submit your work to Neotropical Veterinary and Animal Science (NVAS), an open-access journal published by CSVET Editora (Brazil) that is now accepting manuscripts for its first articles.

NVAS has a broad scope: it covers all areas of veterinary medicine and animal science and all animal species, from basic research to clinical practice, animal production and One Health, with special interest in the Neotropics and other tropical regions.

What we publish

  • Original research articles and short communications
  • Case reports, case series and clinical images
  • Systematic, scoping and narrative reviews
  • Methods and technical notes, data articles and perspectives

Why submit now

  • No fees: there is no submission fee, and no article processing charge for manuscripts accepted until 31 January 2027.
  • Double-anonymous peer review by at least two reviewers, following COPE, ICMJE and EQUATOR guidance.
  • Immediate open access under CC BY 4.0; authors retain copyright.
  • Continuous publication: each article is published online as soon as it is ready.
