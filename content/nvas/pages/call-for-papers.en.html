Article types

We welcome work from every field of veterinary medicine and animal science, on any animal species. Limits refer to the main text.

  • Original research articleExperimental, observational, clinical, diagnostic or field study.up to 6,000 words
  • Short communicationNew, preliminary or confirmatory findings of immediate interest.up to 2,500 words
  • Case report or case seriesA novel case (or outbreak) with diagnostic or therapeutic lessons, or three or more related cases.up to 2,500 / 3,500 wordsgreat for students
  • Clinical imageClinical photo, radiology, endoscopy, necropsy or slide with a confirmed diagnosis and a short text, submitted in English or Portuguese and published in both. See the call for imagesup to 500 words per languagegreat for students
  • ReviewSystematic review or meta-analysis, scoping, narrative or mini-review.3,000 to 8,000 words
  • Method or technical noteTechnique, protocol, diagnostic test, device or software, with validation data.up to 3,500 words
  • Data articleDescription of a dataset deposited in a public repository.up to 2,500 words
  • Perspective or commentaryEvidence-based opinion on technology, education, policy or practice.up to 2,500 words

How it works

From registration to publication everything happens online, and you follow each step in your dashboard.

  1. RegisterCreate your free account on the journal system.
  2. PrepareFollow the guidelines: text in English or Portuguese, figures and graphs with text in English, anonymized manuscript and title page. First article? Use the templates with step-by-step guidance. The author declaration is completed online when you submit.
  3. SubmitClick Submit manuscript, upload your files and fill in the details in a few steps.
  4. Editorial screeningUsually within 7 days: scope, ethics, format and similarity check.
  5. Peer reviewAt least two expert reviewers assess the work without knowing who the authors are.
  6. Decision and revisionYou receive the reports and send the revised version with your response to reviewers.
  7. TranslationIf the article was accepted in Portuguese, the journal translates it into English and you review and approve it.
  8. PublicationCopyediting, proofs and your article online with a DOI, open to the whole world.

Before you submit

  • The journal publishes in English, but you may submit in Portuguese. Review is carried out in the language of submission; if the article is accepted, the journal translates it into English free of charge and you approve the translation. Figures and graphs must already be in English at submission: the journal does not redraw figures. Clinical images: submitted in English or Portuguese, published in both.
  • Students are welcome, with their supervisor or another graduate professional as responsible co-author.
  • Ethics: animal ethics committee approval for animal studies; routine clinical cases need no ethics approval, and a consent form from the legal guardian only if the animal or its guardian can be identified.
  • Author declaration online: in the last step of the submission you tick each item on behalf of all authors. Nothing to print or sign.
